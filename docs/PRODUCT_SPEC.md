# InkFlow Studio — Product Specification

## 1. Product Vision
InkFlow Studio is a tablet-first, offline-first digital notebook, PDF annotator, infinite whiteboard, and study workspace designed for Android tablets (including stylus devices like S-Pen, Lenovo Precision Pen, Xiaomi Smart Pen, and standard capacitive styluses).

It delivers a calm, professional, high-performance workspace where users own their data completely without forced cloud accounts, subscription lock-ins, or privacy invasions.

---

## 2. Target Personas & Primary Use Cases
1. **Students & Researchers:** Taking handwritten lecture notes, annotating research papers (PDFs), recording synchronized audio lectures, generating flashcards, and reviewing with spaced repetition.
2. **Professionals & Engineers:** Meeting minutes with audio context, architectural diagrams on infinite whiteboards, PDF contract markup and signing.
3. **Educators:** Creating structured notebook pages, grading student PDF assignments with colored digital pens, creating review quizzes.

---

## 3. Product Modes
- **Notebook Mode:** Fixed-page or continuous scrolling multi-page notebooks with customizable paper templates (Ruled, Cornell, Grid, Dotted, Blank).
- **PDF Markup Mode:** Native PDF reader with high-resolution vector pen/highlighter annotation overlays, page thumbnails, and PDF export.
- **Infinite Whiteboard Mode:** Non-paginated 2D infinite canvas for freehand brainstorming, sticky notes, cards, and diagrams.
- **Study & Review Mode:** Flashcard deck manager with SM-2 spaced repetition review sessions and self-assessment quizzes.

---

## 4. Key Workflows
- **Flow A: Quick Note Creation:** Open app -> Tap '+' -> Choose 'New Notebook' -> Select template & title -> Immediately begin handwriting -> Automatic local saving.
- **Flow B: PDF Import & Annotation:** Open app -> Tap 'Import PDF' -> Select PDF via system file picker -> View pages -> Annotate with stylus -> Tap 'Export' -> Save flattened/composite PDF.
- **Flow C: Lecture Recording:** In Notebook -> Tap 'Record Audio' -> Write notes as professor speaks -> Stop recording -> Tap any stroke later to hear audio from that exact moment.
- **Flow D: AI Summaries & Flashcards:** Open 'Ask InkFlow' panel -> Tap 'Generate Summary' or 'Create Flashcards' -> Review generated flashcards -> Save to Study Workspace -> Start spaced repetition practice.
